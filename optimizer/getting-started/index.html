Your first search in nine steps

For IMG Pro members. What IMG Optimizer does, how to run a search and how to read what it finds.

Open IMG Optimizer

You need your IMG Pro access code and a TradingView chart with IMG Pro on it.

01STEP 1 OF 9

It tests settings on past candles

IMG Optimizer tests many IMG Pro setting combinations on a market’s past prices (a backtest). Every dot is one test. The best become ready-made settings.

02STEP 2 OF 9

Open a market

Sign in with your IMG Pro access code. Search for a market, or tap one of the suggestions. Members may have found settings already, so look first.

TAPExplore

03STEP 3 OF 9

Pick a style and a goal

On the market page, tap START A FRESH SEARCH. Style sets which chart timeframes are tested. Goal picks the trade-off between return and drop.

TAPSTART A FRESH SEARCH

04STEP 4 OF 9

Tune it, if you want

Search depth is how many settings it tries for each timeframe pair: 64 is quick, 512 takes about eight times longer. Minimum completed trades: 10 to 200.

TAPShow expert options

05STEP 5 OF 9

Start it, then walk away

It runs on the server and takes hours. Close the page if you like. Keep it open and tap Notify me when done for a browser alert.

TAPStart search

06STEP 6 OF 9

Read the numbers

Return is the total gain over the tested dates. Biggest drop is the worst fall from a peak. These are past results, not a forecast.

TAPView the best settings

07STEP 7 OF 9

Check it was steady

Look at the curve: steady settings climb in many small steps; a single jump may be one lucky trade. Check Trades, compare Long-term with Recent.

TAPLong-termRecent

08STEP 8 OF 9

Tweak an existing result

Open any settings and tap Refine for me. IMG Optimizer searches nearby settings on the last 3 or 6 months. Compare, then choose.

TAPRefine for meStart recent update

09STEP 9 OF 9

Put it on your chart

In Put these on your chart, choose TradingView and work down the four steps. Then tap Mark as on my chart.

TAPMark as on my chart

Worth knowing

Three plain facts

Past, not future
Backtest

Every number is how settings behaved on past prices. It can’t promise how they’ll do next.

Checks every setting must pass
60 trades

The default minimum, plus a positive return and results that hold up across separate stretches of history.

Shared work
Already done

If the same search finished recently, you see its settings at once. If it is running now, you can follow it.
